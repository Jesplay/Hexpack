# Mote Nexus & Motes (Hexal)

## What it is
A **Mote Nexus** is Hexal's *magic storage block*. Hexes can turn items into **motes**: an item stack "mediafied" into an iota you can carry on the stack. The items themselves live **inside the Nexus**; the mote on your stack is just a *pointer* to that record. Copies of a mote all point to the same items.

One Nexus holds **1023 different records**, each with **unlimited items**. Hexes can then count, split, merge, craft with, trade with and drop those items, which makes it the basis of automated item handling.

## How to get one
It's **not craftable**. You make it with [[Flay Mind]]:

| Need | Details |
|---|---|
| Block | a **Shulker Box** (any colour) placed in the world |
| Villager | a **Cartographer** of level **2 (Apprentice)** or higher |
| Spell | [[Flay Mind]] (Great Spell, needs **enlightenment**) |
| Cost | 10 Charged Amethyst |

If you're stuck because Flay Mind won't work, you aren't enlightened yet. Follow **[[Progression - Enlightenment & Great Spells]]** step by step.

## First steps once you have one
1. **Bind to it.** Look at the Nexus and cast: opener ([[Mind's Reflection]] → [[Compass' Purification]] → [[Mind's Reflection]] → [[Alidade's Purification]]) → [[Archer's Distillation]] → [[Bind Storage]]. That costs 32 dust, once. From now on, items you mediafy go into *this* Nexus.
2. **Turn an item into a mote.** Drop an item at your feet: [[Mind's Reflection]] → [[Compass' Purification II]] → [[Entity Purification Item]] → [[Mediafy Item]]. A mote is now on your stack.
3. **Keep the mote.** Save it to a [[Focus]] with [[Scribe's Gambit]] so you can get at those items later.
4. **Get items back.** Mote + a position → [[Return Item]] drops them there (optionally only *n* of them).
