# Spectrum

> **Category:** [[Cross-Mod Compatibility (Category)|Cross-Mod Compatibility]]  
> **Source mod:** [[YAHA]]

I can use my [[Conjure Anvil|Conjured Anvil]] to crush certain ingredients, just like with a regular anvil. However, unlike the dense, iron anvils that crush more items at greater heights, my conjured anvils rely on greater velocities instead.
