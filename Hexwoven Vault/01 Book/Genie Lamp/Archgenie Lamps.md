# Archgenie Lamps

> **Category:** [[Genie Lamp (Category)|Genie Lamp]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

The [[Hand Lamps|Hand Lamp]] *merged with* the sacrifice. I can feel it still breathing. I shall refer to my creation as an **Archgenie Lamp**.

Now that I've.. upgraded the processing component of my **Lamp**, it requires far less supervision: I can simply command it to be active and while in my inventory, they will cast every instant, as though I were always holding down on a [[Hand Lamps|Hand Lamp]].

The genie stops casting when it determine when it is in the hands of new casters. No one can force me to cast.

*I am a great soft jelly thing. Smoothly rounded, with no mouth, with pulsing white holes filled by fog where my eyes used to be. Rubbery appendages that were once my arms; bulks rounding down into legless humps of soft slippery matter.*

*I have no mouth. And I must scream.*

When an **Archgenie Lamp** is turned off, it casts an additional time. Like with a regular [[Hand Lamps|Hand Lamp]], I can use [[Finale Reflection]] to recognize whether a cast is that last burst of energy.
