# Caduceus Changes

> **Category:** [[Getting Started (Category)|Getting Started]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Caduceus]]

I have noticed some changes to the behavior of certain [[Iris' Gambit|meta-evaluation techniques]] as of late.

Jump [[Iotas|iotas]] are no longer entirely opaque to me - I can now see the [[Jump Manipulation|components]] that make them up, although I have not yet managed to isolate one.

Additionally, it seems [[Mishaps|Too Many Iotas]] [[Mishaps|mishaps]] have become more common when working with Jump iotas. I should take care not to delve too deep.
