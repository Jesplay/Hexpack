# Hexical Changes

> **Category:** [[Getting Started (Category)|Getting Started]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

I feel strangely more in tune with the ambient **[[Media|media]]** around me. It feels *friendlier* somehow. I can even feel it rippling around me, responding to my actions, as if engaging with me. The **media** also feels more malleable, more *workable*; I sense I am able to manipulate it into more precise and specific specialized effects. I can also bring up my **Hex Notebook** by pressing hexical.open_hexbook**.**

I also find my tools more familiar. I don't require the same level of caution with my [[Staves|Staff]] and can move around during casting, handy for chasing down targets that get out of my line of sight before I can finish my raycast. I shouldn't become too inattentive though, Nature has not become more forgiving of my miscalculations. I can also write directly into my [[Abacus]] with [[Scribe's Gambit]].

**Lightning Rod Staff**: shaped crafting

`A` = Amethyst Block · `L` = Lightning Rod

By fixing a **Block of Amethyst** to some **Lightning Rods**, I can make quite the impressive [[Staves|Staff]]. It is quite weighty to wield but feels absolutely *electrifying* to use.
