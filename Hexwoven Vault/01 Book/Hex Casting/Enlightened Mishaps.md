# Enlightened Mishaps

> **Category:** [[Hex Casting (Category)|Hex Casting]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Casting]]

I have discovered new and horrifying modes of failure. I must not succumb to them.

### Inert Mindflay

Attempted to flay the mind of something that I have either already used, or of a character not suitable for the target block.

Causes dark green sparks. Subjects I have already used will be killed outright, while those that are simply unsuitable will be slightly damaged.

### Lack Spell Circle

Tried to cast an action requiring a [[Spell Circles|spell circle]] without a spell circle.

Causes light blue sparks, and upends my inventory onto the ground.

### Lack Akashic Record

Tried to access an [[Akashic Libraries|Akashic Record]] at a location where there isn't one.

Causes purple sparks, and steals away some of my experience.

### Improper Shepherding

Tried to activate a [[Directrices|Shepherd Directrix]] without a boolean on the top of the [[The Stack|stack]].

Causes red and white sparks, and forcibly ejects the Shepherd Directrix from the circle. This doesn't destroy it, thankfully, but I should make sure to go pick it up and replace it where it belongs.
