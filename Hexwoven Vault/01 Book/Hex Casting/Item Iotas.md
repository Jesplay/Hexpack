# Item Iotas

> **Category:** [[Hex Casting (Category)|Hex Casting]]  
> **Source mod:** [[MoreIotas]]

I have discovered two additional types of [[Iotas|iotas]] I can manipulate.

 Item Type Iotas describe a singular item stripped of its properties, such as damage or enchantments.

 Item [[The Stack|Stack]] Iotas describe a stack of items as they might appear in my inventory, amount, custom names, enchantments, and all.

It would also seem Item Stack iotas referring to [[Focus|Foci]] containing other Item Stack iotas are somewhat faulty. A limit to the depth of my thought perhaps.
