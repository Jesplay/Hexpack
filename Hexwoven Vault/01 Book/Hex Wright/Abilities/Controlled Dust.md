# Controlled Dust

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Abilities (Category)|Abilities]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

The most advanced power orb gives it's wearer control over Amethyst Dust. The orb has hundreds of thousands of Hex Patterns etched into the inner surface and exposes a set of simple patterns for the user to easily control thousands of individual dust particles.

### Density

Packed tight, you can stand on dust, it's solid, it holds back water and it hurts to be hit by it. Spread too thin and you lose these properties.
It all depends on how much dust you use and the size of the shape you conform it to.

### Cost

Dust is lost whenever it does something, like striking things, carrying things or holding things trapped inside.

When this happens, your dust is topped up from your [[Media|media]].

If you take the orb off or leave the dimension, you lose the dust.

`num` → `—`

Spread dust into the air and seize control of it. Max amount is 500 amethyst dust.

Once dust is released, you never get it back, it's spent.

If you lower the dust amount, the effect is instant as control is dropped for the difference in dust, but if you increase the amount, it takes time to flow from you to the formation. So increasing the amount isn't a way to instantly patch a damaged formation.

`region` → `—`

Gives the dust a [[Regions|region]] to fill. The whole region must be within your ambit.

Formed dust is solid to everyone, you included.

Forming any other region, even the same shape somewhere else, reforms the dust. To move a formation without reforming, use Impel or Move.

Formations more than 48 blocks from the caster returns to dust.

`vector` → `—`

Carries the formation until its centre is at the position. Acceleration is dependant on size and distance.

Anything standing on a moving formation is carried along with it.

Impel keeps going until you tell it otherwise. Move stops by itself when it gets there, and the position has to be within your ambit. Impelling a formation whilst moving abandons the move.
