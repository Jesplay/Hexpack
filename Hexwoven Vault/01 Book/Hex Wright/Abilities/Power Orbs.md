# Power Orbs

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Abilities (Category)|Abilities]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

A Power Orb holds a single ability, allowing the wearer to channel it.

Even amongst Hex Wrights, this was advanced and complicated work and the ability to recreate them has been lost to time and studying them shatters the orb.

### Channelling

An orb must be given a sigil, a unique pattern, using [[Scribe's Gambit]].

The Orb must then be placed into the appropriate accessory slot and drawing that pattern activates or toggles that ability.

Orbs can be dyed like leather armour.
