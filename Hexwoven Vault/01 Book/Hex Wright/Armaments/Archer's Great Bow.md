# Archer's Great Bow

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Armaments (Category)|Armaments]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Archer's Great Bow

*Item:* **Masterwork Archer Great Bow**

A heavy bow that can be inscribed with a Hex using [[Engraver's Gambit]]. Any normal arrow fired will be imbued with this hex which will activate on contact with an entity. The entity will be put onto the beginning of the [[The Stack|stack]].

There is a small cooldown between imbuements during which time the arrows fired will behave as normal arrows. Quality level affects the cooldown and damage dealt.
The quality also affects the chance to refund an arrow when firing.
Also affected by the [[Armour & Robes|Vantor Armor]].
