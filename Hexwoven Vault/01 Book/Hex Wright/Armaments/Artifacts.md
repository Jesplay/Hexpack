# Artifacts

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Armaments (Category)|Armaments]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

These are items crafted by the original Hex Wrights using techniques that are still lost to us.
Some are weapons, some grant utility and some contain immense power.
They're can be found in abandoned Hex Wright structures or even powering their creations.

You can keep track of them in your journal.

### Artifact Tablet Fragment

*Item:* **Artifact Tablet Fragment**

Some of how they were made survives, broken up. Fragments of tablets recording how they were made have been found scattered all over the world. It looks like it'll take 6 fragments to make one whole tablet.

### Artifact Tablet

*Item:* **Artifact Tablet**

Reading a mended tablet teaches you how to [[Essence Forge|forge]] an artifact. But be warned, they're expensive to forge.
