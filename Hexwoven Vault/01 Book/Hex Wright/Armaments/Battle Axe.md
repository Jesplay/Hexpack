# Battle Axe

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Armaments (Category)|Armaments]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Battle Axe

*Item:* **Masterwork Battle Axe**

The Battle Hammer's sharper twin, two handed and specialised in slashing. Right click to cut a wide wedge in front of you and fire a crescent energy slice in the direction you're looking.
Inscribe it with [[Engraver's Gambit]] and the

hex fires once the energy dissipates and puts all entities sliced as a list onto the beginning of the [[The Stack|stack]].
