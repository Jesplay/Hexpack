# Battle Hammer

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Armaments (Category)|Armaments]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Battle Hammer

*Item:* **Masterwork Battle Hammer**

A heavy two-handed beast of a weapon, great for clearing room. Right click to slam it into the ground and send a wave rolling outward, knocking back and hurting everything caught in the shockwave radius.

Created with enough Arcane Essence to be able to take a Hex Pattern inscribed by [[Engraver's Gambit]]. Everything caught in the shockwave will be put as a list onto the beginning of the [[The Stack|stack]].


**Sources:** 🧩 [Hexwright on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/DXkdjl8n)
