# Pocket Caster

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Casting Tools (Category)|Casting Tools]]

### Pocket Caster

*Item:* **Pocket Caster**

A handy device that has a slot for a [[Focus|focus]] and a small inventory. Clicking cast will put the items as entities in a list on the top of the [[The Stack|stack]] and cast the hex on the focus.


**Sources:** 🧩 [Hexwright on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/DXkdjl8n)
