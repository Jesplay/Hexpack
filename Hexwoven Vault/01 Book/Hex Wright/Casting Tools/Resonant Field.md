# Resonant Field

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Casting Tools (Category)|Casting Tools]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Resonant Field Marker

*Item:* **Resonant Field Marker**

The Hex Wrights of old were renowned for their huge workshops and their total dominion over them, often casting hexes affecting their workshop hundreds of blocks away.
This is the modern attempt at recreating that.

The Resonant Field Marker allows ambit to be artificially extended by placing 8 of them in a cuboid formation.

The eight corner blocks must be perfectly axis-aligned across all faces of the cuboid.
Max length of any dimension is 200 blocks.

### The Field Tuner

*Item:* **Field Tuner**

Right-click each of the eight markers with the tuner, in any order. The field becomes active on the 8th.

Sneak-click a marker already in a field to remove it from the group. Sneak-click elsewhere to reset the entire tuner.

### The Resonant Field

Whenever you stand within an active Resonant Field, your ambit will be the entirety of the inside of the field.

Your personal ambit still exists also, so standing by the edge of a field, you'd still be able to influence things in your range outside it.
