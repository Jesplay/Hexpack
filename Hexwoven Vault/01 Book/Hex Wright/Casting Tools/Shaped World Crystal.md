### Shaped World Crystal

*Item:* **Shaped World Crystal**

The heart of every Golem, or I should say, the mind of every Golem. They hold a pocket dimensions containing the complex machinery of an artificial mind.
Unfortunately this pocket dimension is served as soon as the Golem is rendered

non-functional but that doesn't mean it is totally inert.
The thousands of ancient hex patterns minutely etched throughout the crystal can be manipulated to exert some influence over space-time. Our lead researcher discovered a pattern that can manipulate the orb into bridging two points in space.

`[vec, vec], [vec, vec], num` → `—`

Takes two sets of vectors forming two planes, folding them to occupy the same space time for the

amount of time specified in num seconds. This gives the effect of a window which can be stepped through in either direction.
It doesn't come without limits though as each plane must match in size and must exist (be loaded) in the world. It will [[Mishaps|mishap]] if not.
