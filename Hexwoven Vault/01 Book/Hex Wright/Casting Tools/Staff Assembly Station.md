# Staff Assembly Station

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Casting Tools (Category)|Casting Tools]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Staff Assembly Station

*Item:* **Staff Assembly Station**

Build and customise your own [[Staves|staff]].
It's more than just a recipe, it takes skill and understanding to customise it to your needs.

### Creating a staff

Everything starts with a staff blank, which on its own, does nothing. The next step is to combine it with a staff core. Staff cores dictate the ability a staff will have.
You can stop here but it's strongly advised you add ingredients to craft a wrap, [[Focus|focus]], and catalyst.

### Attunement

You can add up to 6 ingredients each for the wrap, focus and catalyst. Choosing the right ingredients is key,  the better suited an item is, the more attuned it will be. The attunement is indicated below each component. There is a max attunement per component and for the entire staff.

### Efficiency

Efficiency is a measure of how suited the ingredients used for components are. A component might give you a good attunement score but it might not be the best you could use, so whilst you might have brute forced things, it isn't very efficient.

### Quality

Quality dictates the finished level of the staff. A staff can be a Masterwork, Exquisite, Fine, Sound or Crude.
Quality is derived from a high attunement score and a high efficiency score. A low score in either can bring down your overall quality.
