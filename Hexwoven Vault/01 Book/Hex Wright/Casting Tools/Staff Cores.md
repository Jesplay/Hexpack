# Staff Cores

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Casting Tools (Category)|Casting Tools]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### The Heart of the Staff

A core is the heart of all staffs, each one giving it's own unique ability.
Created at the Essence Forge, they range from Masterwork to Crude. The better the quality, the better the effect of the ability.

### Prism Core

*Item:* **Prism Core**

Allows the wielder to create a zone around them which continuously casts whilst channelled. The [[The Stack|stack]] always begins with what's in the zone: \[\[hostiles\], \[animals\], \[items\], \[players\]\], null list if none.
Narrow with [[Wielder's Gambit]].

### Traveller Core

*Item:* **Traveller Core**

Gives the wielder more affinity for [[Greater Teleport]], discounting it's cost and preventing any accidental spillages. Only applies to the wielder or items. No discounts or protection for other entities or players.

### Scribe Core

*Item:* **Scribe Core**

It's internal structure resembles that of a Hex Caster with the greatest of memories.
This core gives the wielder access to a Hexicon. Simply equip the [[Staves|staff]] and press the bound Hexicon key.

[[Wielder's Gambit]] has no effect on this core.

### Bolt Core

*Item:* **Bolt Core**

Gives a staff the ability to fire an energised Hex Bolt. Bolt has an ambit at impact point, which is affected by core quality. The stack begins with: \[impact vec, \[entities caught\]\].
Hex is written to the staff using [[Engraver's Gambit]].

### Mnemonic Core

*Item:* **Mnemonic Core**

A repository for great spells, easily accessible when casting.
[[Wielder's Gambit]] takes a number corresponding to the slot and deletes the stored Great Spell. Left click with scroll and staff in hand to store.

### Cores and Effect

Quartz: Sets the size of the zone. No narrower than 4, and no wider than the core max. [[Mishaps|Mishaps]] otherwise.

Mnemonic: num is the the slot of a Great Spell to erase, top of the list is 0, next down is 1, then 2, etc.
