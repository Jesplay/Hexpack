# Void Pocket

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Casting Tools (Category)|Casting Tools]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Pocketable Void Pocket

*Item:* **Pocketable Void Pocket**

Created at the [[Essence Forge]], this little device is a self contained tiny pocket universe with just enough storage for 25 casting related items if crafted skillfully. Each grade grants 5 slots. Think of it as a hotbar for your hotbar.

### Quick Draw

Hold your Void Pocket Overlay key with a Void Pocket. Whatever slot your mouse is over when you release the key is the item it'll be switched to. You can only add hex related items, but this lets you quickly switch between different things without having to rifle through your inventory.

### Accidental Loss

We all know how easy it is to accidentally lose a pocket universe, that's why this one was created with a homing feature.
If you lose your Void Pocket whilst it has an item selected, through accidental lava interaction, placement of the currently selected item, or any other numerous ways, then the Void pocket will eject the item

it's holding and use that spare energy to return to you.

A word of warning though, if you don't have an item selected, then the Void Pocket and all its contents will be lost or destroyed.
