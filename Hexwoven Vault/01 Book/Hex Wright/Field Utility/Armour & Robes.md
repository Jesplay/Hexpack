# Armour & Robes

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Field Utility (Category)|Field Utility]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Armour & Robes

*Item:* **Masterwork Cantor Chestplate**

There are six armour types currently. Augur,Hexwarden, Cantor, Veilwalker, Venator and Domitor.

Each has a unique ability and 4 quality grades, from Sound to Masterwork.

### Abilites

You must wear at least 3 pieces of the same set for the abilities to work, and one piece must be a chest plate.

The pieces need not match in quality - a mixed suit works at the quality of its weakest piece.

### Augur

*Item:* **Masterwork Augur Chestplate**

The Augur armour bestows the power of foresight.
When drawing patterns you will be shown the next possible steps and pattern names.

Requires Augur Gem to craft

### Hexwarden

*Item:* **Masterwork Hexwarden Chestplate**

The Hexwarden armour wards the wearer against [[Mishaps|mishaps]].
An amount of damage, blindness, XP loss, dropped or drowned is soaked up by the plate before it reaches you: 25% at Sound, up to 100%.

### Cantor

*Item:* **Masterwork Cantor Chestplate**

The Cantor armour gives the wearer their own additional ravenminds. A new pattern gives them up to 4 additional Ravenminds, depending on the quality of the armour. Masterworks giving 4 and Sound giving 1.

### Veilwalker

*Item:* **Masterwork Veilwalker Chestplate**

The Veilwalker armour is sewn with hidden pockets, and carries more talismans.
It adds a talisman slot per quality: 1 at Sound, up to 4 at Masterwork on top of the existing 3.
Requires Veilwalker Gem to craft

### The Veilwalker's Pockets

Taking it off does not empty the pockets but it does disable the Talismans in the extra slots. Taking those Talismans out the disabled slot will result in that slot disappearing until a Veilwalker Chestplate is worn again. It will also enable any disabled Talismans.

### Venator

*Item:* **Masterwork Venator Chestplate**

The Venator armour was designed to compliment Hex Wrought bows. It shortens the bow's hex recharge by up to 40% at Masterwork with up to 90% chance to return an arrow.

Requires a Venator Crystal to craft.

### Domitor

*Item:* **Masterwork Domitor Chestplate**

The Domitor Chestplate is tempered against fire and blasts. It absorbs a portion of any explosions or fire damage: 25% at Sound, 50%, 75%, and total immunity at Masterwork.

Requires a Domitor Gem to craft
