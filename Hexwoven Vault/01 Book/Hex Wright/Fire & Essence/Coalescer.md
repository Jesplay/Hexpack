# Coalescer

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Fire & Essence (Category)|Fire & Essence]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

**Coalescer**: shaped crafting

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
| `A` | `G` | `A` |
| `I` | `S` | `I` |
| `I` | `I` | `I` |

`A` = Amethyst Shard · `G` = Kyanite · `I` = Iron Ingot · `S` = Smoker

The opposite to throwing things into a [[Crucible|crucible]] to burn.
This crafts items from Essences.
Not every item is craftable though.

### The Wizards' Share

Turning items into Essence always results in some Essence loss. This has been given the nickname 'The Wizards' Share'.
It will always cost more to make an item than what you get from the item in the Crucible.

### Priming the Coalescer

The Coalescer requires an example item to know what to make. The item isn't consumed, it's just a blueprint, or the seed, as it's known.
