`I` = Iron Ingot · `A` = Amethyst Shard · `F` = Anvil

Dock an [[Endless Pouch]] or [[Resonant Key]], choose what to make and hone your skills as you forge hex casting implements.

### Forging

Each Essence needs to be infused by striking the forge.  Striking when the needle crosses the brightest zone is a perfect strike.
Your accuracy across every round decides the final quality. Each round reduces the strike zone and the needle moves faster.

Not every recipe is available straight away, the must be unlocked by finding knowledge left behind on [[Stone Tablet|Stone Tablets]]
