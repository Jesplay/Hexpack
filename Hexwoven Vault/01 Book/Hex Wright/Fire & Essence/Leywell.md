`B` = Amethyst Block · `A` = Amethyst Shard · `G` = Kyanite · `S` = Smooth Stone

The Leywell is my poor imitation of the devices built by our ancestors created by studying the few remaining texts of the Hex Wright masters. It extracts Essence from the surrondings.

Placed Leywells do nothing until they're attuned, at which point they passively extract Essence from their surroundings. My initial test was in a forest which yielded me Organic Essence.
