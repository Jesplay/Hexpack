# Broom & Carpet

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Flying Devices (Category)|Flying Devices]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Broom

*Item:* **Broom**

Built for speed, brooms were the quick method of long haul travel favoured by Hex Casters of old.

They're ridden like any mount, but powered by hex.

Quality affects max acceleration & speed.

### Flying Carpet

*Item:* **Flying Carpet**

Steadier than a broom and no slower, this was the preferred way of travelling long distance in comfort.

Seats two or a driver and a chest, and holds a deeper reservoir.

Quality affects max acceleration & speed.

Carpets can have chests mounted to them by using a chest on a carpet. It can be accessed by using the chest.

To remove a chest, sneak and right-click when the chest is empty.

### Found Brooms

Most brooms are struck at the Essence Forge, and fly by the grade they were struck at.

Three are not. **Starbeam**, **Nightsweep** and **Twin Whisk** are only ever found - in the deep places, and in what a quartz golem hoards. No tablet names them and no strike makes them.

They outfly anything the Forge can produce, and they carry no grade at all.

Some brooms were built for company. **Starbeam**, **Nightsweep** and the forged **Gleamglide** each carry one behind the pilot; the **Twin Whisk** has a bench either side of the shaft and carries two. Passengers cost the broom lift.
