# Essence

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Getting Started (Hex Wright) (Category)|Getting Started]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

Something we'd long suspected but have now confirmed is the existence of Essence! This is the Physical energy counterpart to the Mental energy we've long been using to shape this world. It's present in everything! The true discovery is that it can be extracted using a [[Crucible|crucible]].

### Endless Pouch

*Item:* **Endless Pouch**

This appears to be the main way Essence was stored. A [[Endless Pouch|simple leather pouch]] inscribed with numerous runes designed to absorb and distribute the essence given to it. It feels odd to be handling energy like it's a physical object.
