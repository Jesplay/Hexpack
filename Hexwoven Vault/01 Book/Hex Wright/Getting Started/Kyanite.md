# Kyanite

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Getting Started (Hex Wright) (Category)|Getting Started]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

Our first discovery, clusters of Kyanite crystal growing from the rock. It's a fascinating substance, and apparently quite rare.
It's very different from Amethyst and Lapis and it appears that it's not a source of mental energy but bridges mental energy to the physical realm.

Interestingly it only grows in small clusters in the caves besides sauces of heat deep underground. Apparently it's love for hot places has even caused very small amounts of it to spontaneously cross over into the Nether.
This substance is one of the pillars of a Hex Wrights' craft.

### Budding Kyanite

*Item:* **Budding Kyanite**

Every cluster sprouts from a block of **Budding Kyanite**, which slowly grows fresh clusters over time. It looks tricky to move without fracturing it into a million pieces.
