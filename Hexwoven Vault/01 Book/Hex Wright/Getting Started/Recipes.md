# Recipes

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Getting Started (Hex Wright) (Category)|Getting Started]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

We currently only have a few examples of Recipes for the [[Essence Forge]] as we're still discovering the lost knowledge of the Hex Wrights but [[Stone Tablet|Stone tablets]] seem to be the best documentation of their creations so far. We've had luck sourcing some from ruined workshops but

the best source we've found are things called Bindstones that are found in large caverns underground. They don't seem to be very stable but they have enough energy to impart some knowledge when interacted with.
