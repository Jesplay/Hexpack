# Qualities

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Hex Wrighting (Category)|Hex Wrighting]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

A lot of what a Hex Wright crafts is complex, it requires patience and skill. This is reflected in the Quality of the item produced.
The better quality an item, the better its effect or stats are.

There are 5 levels of quality that can be achieved and these determine the finals effects of an item.

Masterwork
Exquisite
Fine
Sound
Crude
