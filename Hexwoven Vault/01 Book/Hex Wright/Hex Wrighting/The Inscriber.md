# The Inscriber

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Hex Wrighting (Category)|Hex Wrighting]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Inscriber

*Item:* **Inscriber**

Used to etch the Hex Wrights signature into their creations.
First use lets you imprint your signature. Careful, once it's imprinted, it can't be redone.

### Etch Your Work

Once it's imprinted, hold the Inscriber in the main hand and right-click anything you've crafted in the off hand like a [[Staves|staff]], a core, or a broom, and it etches your mark.
