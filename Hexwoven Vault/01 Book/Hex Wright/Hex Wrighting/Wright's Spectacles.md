# Wright's Spectacles

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Hex Wrighting (Category)|Hex Wrighting]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

Worn by all the great Hex Wrights of the past, these specs allow you to see the properties of a Hex Wrights workshop. You can see info like the perimeter of a [[Warding Box]], the channel and [[Media|media]] of an [[Harmonic Emitter|Emitter]], the name of a [[Resonance Tower]]. Any properties like these.

A Wright does need to not choose between the fashionable specs and a Scrying Lens. Simply mash combine the spectacles with a lens in a crafting table and the lens is fitted into the frame. You get the best of both worlds!
