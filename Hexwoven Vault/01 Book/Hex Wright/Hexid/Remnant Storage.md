# Remnant Storage

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Hexid (Category)|Hexid]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

A tank is bulk storage for [[The Reaping|remnants]]. It holds 2000 rems per block, so a six high tank holds 12,000, with no degradation.

Remnants can only be added to empty tanks or tanks containing the same remnant.

### Filling

Right click the tank with a filled [[The Hex Engraved Bottle|Hex Engraved Bottle]] to pour it in.

[[Chronicler's Gambit]] aimed at a tank can also add Remnants to the tank.

Mixed Remnant tanks can't be created by eiter method.

### Extracting

Right click with an empty Hex Engraved bottle to fill it up to that bottle's capacity.

You can't mix Remnants, the bottle must either be empty or contain the Remnant you're extracting.
