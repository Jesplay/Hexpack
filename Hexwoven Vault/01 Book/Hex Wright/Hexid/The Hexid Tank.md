# The Hexid Tank

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Hexid (Category)|Hexid]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Hexid Tank

*Item:* **Hexid Tank**

A tank holds either Hexid or [[Remnant Storage|remnants]] suspensions.

Tanks can be built up to to 6 blocks high. Each block holds 4000 mB.

### Hexid

Hexid is water with [[Media|media]] dissolved in it. Fill a tank with water buckets, then right click it with anything holding media to dissolve that media in. Saturation is how close the tank is to its ceiling of 1 dust per mB, so a full six high tank tops out at 24,000 dust.

Recharge Item cast at a tank adds media.

### Learning It

Tanks aren't on any [[Stone Tablet|tablet]]; you learn them by standing in a room that still has the plumbing running.

One place does: the corrupt laboratory buried in a lost workshop. Walk into it and both recipes appear at the [[Essence Forge]].

Neither is graded, so there's no timing round: pay the essence and take the result. Pipes come 8 to a craft.

### Plumbing

Hexid Pipes join every tank they reach into one vessel.

Sneak place a pipe to lay it in a straight line instead of joining to compatible blocks around it, like other pipes.

Tanks holding different things won't mix and the pipes will break if attempted.
