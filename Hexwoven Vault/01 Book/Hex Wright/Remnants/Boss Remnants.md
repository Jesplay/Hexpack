# Boss Remnants

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Remnants (Category)|Remnants]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### A Specific Thing

Bosses are unique creatures, with unique abilities, so their Remnants are always the most prized.
Make sure you bring a good quality [[The Hex Engraved Bottle|bottle]]with you, as their Rems are roughly 180, far too much for a crude or sound bottle.

### Remnant Effects

- Shardskin from  the Quartz Golem. Attackers are hit with quartz
- Adamant from the Ancient Golem. Makes you highly resistant to almost all damage, but also weighs you down.
- Blight from a Wither. Your hits give blight.
- Ascendant from the Ender Dragon. Gives flight.

### Remnant Effects

- Tremor from the Warden. Everything alive within 24 blocks glows, through any amount of stone.
- Interdict from an Elder Guardian. Hostiles within 12 blocks are slowed and weakened.
