# The Hex Engraved Bottle

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Remnants (Category)|Remnants]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Hex Engraved Bottle

*Item:* **Hex Engraved Bottle**

A Thin Hex Etched glass bottles created at the [[Essence Forge]] to hold a remnant. The better the [[Qualities|quality]]of the bottle, the greater the amount of rems, a measure of potency, can be held. A bottle can only hold one type of remnant at a time.

Generally a mobs remnant will have about 100 rems, which is the equivalent of 1 crude bottle.

### Filling

A bottle can be filled by writing a [[Distillation|Remnant Iota]] to it using scribes gambit or chronicler's gambit. The Hex inscribed in the glass will then convert this into a stable Hexid (Hex Fluid). To empty it, just write null to the bottle. If you write a greater amount that it can hold, the excess will be lost.

### Degredation

Make sure you store your Remnant in a bottle or tank as they will eventually degrade. You get 5 minutes at full strength and then an hour, during which it will degrade to nothing. Once stored they will last indefinitely.

### Bottoms Up!

We're unsure if the original Hex Wrights ever did the same experiment that one of our lead researchers did.
They discovered that by drinking these Remnant Hexids, they could temporarily adopt their traits! Through rigerous experimentation it was determined that 10 Rems equals 1 second.

### Placing Bottles

Sneak right clicking lets you place the bottle on at the angle you're facing on a block. If the block is destroyed, via any means, the bottle turns into an item available to be picked up.
