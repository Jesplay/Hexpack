# The Reaping 

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Remnants (Category)|Remnants]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

In their pursuit of creating Golems, Hex Wrights discovered that the physical traits and abilities of monsters could be stripped from them at the point of death, when their mind relinquished it's grip.
These remnants were then infused into atificial stone bodies to give them traits.

`entity` → `remnants`

Tears everything a fresh corpse has to give into a Remnants [[Iotas|iota]].
Costs 1 Dust.

### Freshness is Key

You can only reap the recently deceased and you'll get a [[Mishaps|mishap]] if you use this pattern on the living, long dead or a corpse that's already been reaped.
There's only a small window of a second before the bodies energy is too dissipated.

### Storing Remnants

Remnants can be stored on any casting medium that takes an iota however extraction is not a perfect process and remnants will degrade over time, becoming less potent. The best way to make full use of them is to distill them straight away.
