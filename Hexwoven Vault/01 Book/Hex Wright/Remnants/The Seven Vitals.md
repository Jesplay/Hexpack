# The Seven Vitals

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Remnants (Category)|Remnants]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Read Off The Body

Vitals are the physical category of a Remnant. Everything has Vitality, their life force, even the undead. The other 6 vitals depend on the creature itself, some could have might whilst others have impact. They all give different effects when drank.

### Remnant Effects

- Vitality Instantly and heals you, a heart per 40rems
- Might: Strength
- Alacrity: Speed
- Aegis: Resistance
- Ballast: Knockback resistance. 400 Rems gives full immunity
- Impact: Your attack has knockback
- Bound: Jump Boost.
