# The Ten Traits

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Remnants (Category)|Remnants]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Read Off The Life

Traits are different to Vitals. They're Remnants of creatures that reflect what they could do. These Remnants temporarily grant these abilties when [[The Hex Engraved Bottle|drank]].

### Remnant Effects

- Ember: Fire Resistance
- Tidebreath: Water Breathing
- Gravesight: Night Vision
- Levity: Slow Falling
- Venom Your attacks have poison
- Slipstream: Increase swimming speed
- Repast: Restores hunger, then Well Fed

- Rime: You cannot freeze
- Guile: Invisibility
- Eloquence: Grants Hero of the Village, and its discounts
