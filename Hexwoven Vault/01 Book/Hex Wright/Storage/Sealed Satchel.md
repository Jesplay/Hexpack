# Sealed Satchel

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Storage (Category)|Storage]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Sealed Satchel

*Item:* **Sealed Satchel**

A hex powered inventory that can be worn. It has a slot for a held item, and 3 slots for events. When worn, the backpack inventory will show when the player inventory is accessed. Can also be access by holding it and right-clicking.

### The Held Slot

The held slot holds any one item. Whatever sits in it counts as held for the satchel's own hook hexes, ahead of anything you wear or carry. A seal is the usual thing placed here.

Hooks may read the slot, they can never write to it.

### The Hooks

The Three hooks: On Open, In and Out, are fired as the window opens, as something is deposited, and as something is withdrawn. Simplest way to use them is to use the patterns from [[The Vault Seal]] with a seal in the held slot.

The hook slots can take any [[Iotas|iota]] holder.

### Open this odd chest?

[[The Hexbound Chest|Hexbound Chests]] and **Sealed Satchels** funnel their hexes through the person opening them, consuming their [[Media|media]]. The opener is protected against any [[Mishaps|mishaps]] but there's no protection against what the hexes contain, be careful what strange chests you open!

### A brand new look

The satchel can be given a fresh new look using [[Caster's Glamour]]! Use it to cycles through and sport the one you want. It can also be dyed!
