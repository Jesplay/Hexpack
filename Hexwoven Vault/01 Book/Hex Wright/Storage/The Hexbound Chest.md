# The Hexbound Chest

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[Storage (Category)|Storage]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

**Hexbound Chest**: shapeless crafting from Chest, Amethyst Block, Echo Shard

A hex powered chest. It has a slot for a held item, and 3 slots for events.

### The Held Slot

The held slot holds any one item. Whatever sits in it counts as held for the chests own hook hexes, ahead of anything you wear or carry. A seal is the usual thing placed here.

Hooks may read the slot, they can never write to it.

### The Hooks

The Three hooks: On Open, In and Out, are fired as the window opens, as something is deposited, and as something is withdrawn. Simplest way to use them is to use the patterns from [[The Vault Seal]] with a seal in the held slot.

The hook slots can take any [[Iotas|iota]] holder.

### Open this odd chest?

**Hexbound Chests** and [[Sealed Satchel|Sealed Satchels]] funnel their hexes through the person opening them, consuming their [[Media|media]]. The opener is protected against any [[Mishaps|mishaps]] but there's no protection against what the hexes contain, be careful what strange chests you open!
