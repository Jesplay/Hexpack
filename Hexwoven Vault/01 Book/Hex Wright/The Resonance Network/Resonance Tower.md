# Resonance Tower

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[The Resonance Network (Category)|The Resonance Network]]

**Resonance Tower**: shaped crafting

| `A` | `A` | `A` |

`G` = Kyanite · `A` = Amethyst Block · `E` = Ender Pearl

Right click a Resonance Tower with an [[Endless Pouch]] to dock it. All attuned [[Resonant Key|Resonant Keys]] in the area will take and deposit Essence to the attuned Tower and  the docked pouch.

### The Network's Name

Every Tower has a unique resonance and an identifier, like Rowan or Thorn.

That identifier is only a label for easy reference. Handy for seeing what keys and items are tuned to.
It also means you can rename the tower without any attunted keys or items breaking.

### Reading and Naming

Cast [[Chronicler's Purification]] on a vector pointing at a Resonance Tower and it returns a resonance [[Iotas|iota]] which is its' unique id and what the network Keys are attuned to.

Cast [[Chronicler's Gambit]] on that same vector with a string, and the network takes that string as its identifier.
