# Resonant Key

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[The Resonance Network (Category)|The Resonance Network]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Resonant Key

*Item:* **Resonant Key**

Right click a [[Resonance Tower|Resonant Tower]] with a key to attune it. Attuned keys can be slotted into pouch slots and if they're in range of a Resonant Tower they will put or pull Essences from the Tower. They can also tune other devices to a network.


**Sources:** 🧩 [Hexwright on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/DXkdjl8n)
