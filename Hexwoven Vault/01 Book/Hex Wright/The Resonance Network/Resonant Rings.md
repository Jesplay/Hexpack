# Resonant Rings

> **Category:** Hex Wright › [[The Resonance Network (Category)|The Resonance Network]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

### Resonant Ring

*Item:* **Resonant Ring**

Attuned to a Resonance Network by right clicking a [[Resonance Tower|Resonant Tower]] with it. A ring can then subscribe to an [[Harmonic Exchange]]. A ring must be worn in the accessory slot to be connected to the Resonance Network.

Every worn ring attuned to the same tower is on one network. The Harmonic Exchange provides 16 channels to subscribe to.

The ring will cast the hex attached when it receives data broadcast on that channel or the channel's state changes.

### The Ring Bearer

Each ring's hex runs as its own wearer. Their [[Media|media]]. Their ambit. Be careful what pattern you subscribe with.

You can broadcast when you receive data or state but the chain breaks after 1 reply . You cannot build a message loop, ironic given they're rings.
