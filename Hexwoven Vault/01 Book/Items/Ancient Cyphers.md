# Ancient Cyphers

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Casting]]

While adventuring, I've come across what appear to be [[Casting Items|Cyphers]] created by the Hexcasters of the ancient past. These **Ancient Cyphers** work just like the ones I can craft: they can't be recharged, and will break when their reserve of **[[Media|Media]]** runs out. However, unlike my own [[Casting Items|Cyphers]], their decayed structure allows me to glimpse the **Hex** stored within.

### Ancient Cypher

*Item:* **Ancient Cypher**

These may be useful for learning new techniques - who better to teach me than ancient masters of the art?

Alternatively, I could erase the contents of one and store my own **Hex** inside, creating a casting item that clearly displays its own function.
