# Casting Carpets

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

I can weave [[Amethyst]] into any ordinary rug to create this wonderfully comfy rug. Stepping on the carpet, I can feel the **[[Media|media]]** coursing under my feet. It feels disorienting but surprisingly puts me at peace.

I feel very inspired, as if I were wearing a [[Scrying Lens]] although weaker. My **Hexcasting** vision seems to have been expanded.

**Casting Carpet**: shapeless crafting from any wool carpets, Amethyst Dust

*Why is the red carpet red? It can be any color.*
