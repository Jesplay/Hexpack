# Casting Hood

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Phlexiful]]

The light produced in my field of work is taxing on the eyes. The flashes of light are brief, but they linger in my vision.
Taking some inspiration from friends in the trades, I've fashioned together a mask to protect my eyes from the harsh light of **Hexcasting**.
