# Casting Items

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Casting]]

Although the flexibility of casting **Hexes** "on the go" with my [[Staves|Staff]] is quite helpful, it's a huge pain to have to wave it around repeatedly just to accomplish a basic task. If I could save a common **Hex** for later reuse, it would simplify things a lot-- and allow me to share my **Hexes** with friends, too.

To do this, I can craft one of three types of magic items: **Cyphers**, **Trinkets**, or **Artifacts**. All of them hold the patterns of a given **Hex** inside, along with a small battery containing **[[Media|media]]**.

Simply holding one and pressing Right-Click will cast the patterns inside, as if the holder had cast them out of a [[Staves|staff]], using its internal battery.

Each item has its own quirks:

**Cyphers** are fragile, destroyed after their internal **media** reserves are gone, and cannot be recharged;

**Trinkets** can be cast as much as the holder likes, as long as there's enough **media** left, but become useless afterwards until recharged;

**Artifacts** are the most powerful of all-- after their **media** is depleted, they can use [[Amethyst]] from the holder's inventory to pay for the **Hex**, just as I do when casting with a [[Staves|Staff]]. Of course, this also means the spell might consume their mind if there's not enough [[Amethyst]].

Once I've made an empty magic item in a mundane crafting bench, I infuse the **Hex** into it using (what else but) a spell appropriate to the item. [[Crafting Casting Items|I've catalogued the patterns here.]]

Each infusion spell requires an entity and a [[What is a Hex|list of iotas]] to cast on the [[The Stack|stack]]. The entity must be a **media**-holding item entity (i.e. [[Amethyst|amethyst]] dropped on the ground); the entity is consumed and forms the battery.

Usefully, it seems that the **media** in the battery is not consumed in chunks as it is when casting with a [[Staves|Staff]]-- rather, the **media** "melts down" into one continuous pool. Thus, if I store a **Hex** that only costs one [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]]'s worth of media, a [[Amethyst|Charged Crystal]] used as the battery will allow me to cast it 10 times.
