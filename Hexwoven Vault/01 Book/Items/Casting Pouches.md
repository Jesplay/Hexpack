# Casting Pouches

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexic]]

Though a [[Phials of Media|Phial]] is my ultimate goal for **[[Media|media]]** storage, knitting a pouch out of [[Mediaweave|mediaweave]] may help me manage my amethyst better. Only a few cloths are needed to give me some decent capacity. Each pouch holds six slots, and I can extend their capacity to twelve with a bit more weave. A small pouch may go within a large pouch, but neither size can be placed within itself.

*Crafting recipe:* **Small Preferred Bundle** (`hexic:small_preferred_bundle`)

Upon Nature's request for **media**, my pouches will offer themselves and their contents first of all; similarly, if I attempt to [[Recharge Item|Recharge]] a pouch, the inserted **media** will form a 'cloud' of sorts inside, which will settle into any [[Casting Items|trinket]] or similar within. To inhibit this behavior, casting pouches may be sealed by applying **Honeycomb** the same way I'd insert an item. I can later wash off the seal by applying a wet sponge.

Something strange about these pouches I have noticed. I can feel the **media** *hesitant*— *resistant*, even— to leave the pouch. Only the media Nature— no, I— ask for is pulled from the pouch. As amethyst shatters inside, I feel the leftover **media** bouncing, ping-ponging, *trapped* in the walls until it evaporates. A battery within— my beloved [[Phials of Media|Phial]], or an [[Casting Items|Artifact]] of mine, could grab bits of this cloud, with none of the costs [[Recharge Item|directing the clouds myself]] incurs.
