# Contained Splinter

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[HexIntent]]

### Contained Splinter

*Item:* **Splinter Caster**

A contained splinter is a fragile sliver of will anchored to a physical box. It can hold a [[Focus|focus]] and will cast this focus through the contained splinter.

Place the Splinter Caster in a [[Spell Circles|spell circle]] and provide a focus by right clicking it with a focus in hand. It will  Will capture the spell circle wave until execution has finished. A redstone pulse to the caster will forcibly release the circle wave.


**Sources:** 🧩 [HexIntent on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/4G64Rm66)
