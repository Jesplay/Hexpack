# Creative-Only Items

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Casting]]

I have invented some fanciful items that can only be acquired through creative means.

### The Media Cube

*Item:* **The Media Cube**

The **[[Media|Media]] Cube** is a near-limitless source of power, acting as an infinite source of **media** for whatever **Hexes** I wish to cast. It also looks quite tasty. Eating it might perhaps reveal more about the world, possibly delving into spoiler territory...

Additionally, it seems that by renaming a **Media Cube** at an Anvil, I should be able to utilize it to test and understand more about my **Hexes**. If I have somehow acquired Creative power over the world, I might wish to turn that off before attempting any of the following.

### debug media

*Item:* **The Media Cube**

By renaming the **Media Cube** to 'debug media', I will be given feedback in the chat window about the amount of media consumed.
