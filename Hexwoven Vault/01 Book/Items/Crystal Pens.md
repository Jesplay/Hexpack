# Crystal Pens

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexic]]

A pen made of amethyst allows me to sketch patterns in midair as input to my spells. Though I use my pen like a normal [[Staves|staff]], patterns I draw with it are merely stored in an internal list rather than executed.
