# Entity Capsule

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Machina]]

### Entity Capsule

*Item:* **Entity Capsule**

Being able to store hexes into **[[Focus|Foci]]** is really helpful. By expanding this concept to its limit Im now able to store also creatures.

The Entity Capsule allows me to encode entities into [[Media|media]] for storage, and I can decode that same entity again back to its original state.
