# Gun and Bullets

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Machina]]

### Hex Gun

*Item:* **Hex Gun**

Although I can to make [[Casting Items|Cyphers]], [[Casting Items|Trinkets]] and  [[Casting Items|Artifacts]]. It seems like I might have overlooked my options for storing and casting my hexes.

The **Hex Gun** can hold up to 6 bullets and cast spells that are stored in them

Each shot cycling the barrel to the next available bullet.

### Amethyst Bullet

*Item:* **Amethyst Bullet**

The **Amethyst Bullet** seems to work the [[Focus|foci]] do, but it can be cast by the gun directly.

### Target Bullet

*Item:* **Target Bullet**

The **Target Bullet** works similarly, but creates a shimmering projectile that on collision with a entity pushes that entity to the [[The Stack|stack]].

It seems to copy my [[Pigments|pigment]] colors on flight, its §opretty!§r

### Block Bullet

*Item:* **Block Bullet**

The **Block Bullet** works similarly to the target bullet, but instead pushes the block position it hits to the stack

Also interesting to note that casting this way seems to give me a bit of ambit around where projectiles hit, for both the target bullet and this one.
