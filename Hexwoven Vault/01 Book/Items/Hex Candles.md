# Hex Candles

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

When ignited for the first time, either [[Prestidigitation (Book Entry)|magically]] or with a **Flint and Steel**, these delightful little candles have pale white flames. Their flames change to copy the [[Pigments|Pigment]] of anyone who interacts with them. I can also give them a [[Pigments|Pigment]] item directly to change its color.

To extinguish, sneak use.

**Hex Candle**: shapeless crafting from Candle, Amethyst Dust

Like with regular **Candles**, I can place them on **Cakes** as well.

*The doctor found himself in an immense cave lined with countless quivering candles, each representing the duration of a life.*
