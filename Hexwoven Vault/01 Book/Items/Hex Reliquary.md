# Hex Reliquary

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[HexIntent]]

### Hex Reliquary

*Item:* **Hex Reliquary**

The Hex Reliquary copies the contents of a [[Focus|focus]] into itself, in one of five slots, it'll also copy the contents back to an empty focus. It'll use the focus's name as a slot label.

Right click the reliquary with a focus to copy to it, and with an empty to copy from it. An empty hand will rotate the slots. Use a scrying lens to see what slot is selected and the name.


**Sources:** 🧩 [HexIntent on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/4G64Rm66)
