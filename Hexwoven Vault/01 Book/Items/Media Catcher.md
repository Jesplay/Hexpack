# Media Catcher

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Phlexiful]]

I've found that the small cost of some spells can build up to be quite a substantial sum of **[[Media|media]]** over time. With a little bit of elbow grease, and some bolts of [[Media Weave]], I have made something to keep these mounting sums at bay.

The **Media Catcher** Slowly accrues **media**. It can't hold much, so I might find some use in [[Media Catcher Patterns|tuning it]] as to limit the release of **media**.

I suppose it isn't unreasonable to assume there could be trace amounts of **media** about, but even then, what this catches is far too much... Where is it coming from?
