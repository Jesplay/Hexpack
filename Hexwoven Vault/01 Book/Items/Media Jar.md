# Media Jar

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

I can melt down all my [[Amethyst]] in this little jar! Unfortunately, the glass is too thick for me to draw **[[Media|media]]** through it, although I have read tales that ancient **Hexcasters** were able to solve this issue and were able to bottle up liquid **media** in some kind of flask to use in their **Hexes**. However, this **Media Jar** also has some fascinating properties for recycling **media**.

*There, sure enough, she saw the faint translucent outline of something aboout the size of a hen's egg. There was just a touch of color in it, a pale sea-green, soft and shimmering and very beautiful.*

I can dunk items into the **Jar** to fill or imbue it with **media**. Items not designed for the containment of **media** are vulnerable to seepage, allowing the **media** to soak in and *transmute* the item in a variety of unpredictable and surprising ways.

This can be done in-world by using an item on the **Jar**, in my inventory just like a **Bundle**, a **Hopper** to insert and extract items, or the [[Hopper (Book Entry)|Hopper]] spell.

### Make Gold

**?**: transmuting from Copper Ingot

Transforms copper ingots to gold.

### Sadden Obsidian

**?**: transmuting from Obsidian

Infuses **Obsidian** with **media** which leaks out of it, giving it a distinctive crying appearance.

### Calm Obsidian

**?**: transmuting from Crying Obsidian

Returns the **media** from **Crying Obsidian**.

*Therapy! :D*

### Weave Thought-Knot

**?**: transmuting from String

Crafts [[Thought-Knot|Thought-Knots]] by submerging **String** in a large volume of **media** rather than brushing an [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]] on it. A slightly more efficient recipe.

### Release Memory

**?**: transmuting from Thought-Knot

Sucks the **media** out of a [[Thought-Knot]]. Loses **media** compared to the crafting recipe, but useful for recycling the little bit of **media**.

### Coal

**?**: transmuting from Charcoal

Transforms **Charcoal** into **Coal** by infusing thought into it and then immediately killing it. Good for compacting.

### Charcoal

**?**: transmuting from Coal

Transforms **Coal** into **Charcoal**. Why?
