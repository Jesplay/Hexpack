# Media Logs

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

**Hexcasting** is an Art just waiting for me to lose track of my casting and Nature is all too ready to punish my carelessness the moment it happens.

Fortunately, Nature also provides a method for me to reflect on my folly... only after suffering the consequences of them, of course.

**Media Log**: shapeless crafting from Amethyst Shard, Edified Log

They call it the **Error [[Media|Media]] Log**. I simply stab a shard of [[Amethyst]] through an [[Edified Trees|Edified Log]] and for some reason, Nature is now ready to spill my errors straight into my mind.

The **Media Log** will only capture mistakes cast by my [[Staves|Staff]] and the [[Casting Items|casting devices]] I use.

By holding it tightly and meditating on it, it reveals to me the last handful of patterns cast sequentially (even by other [[Meta-Evaluation|patterns]], all flattened out into a linear stream), the [[Mishaps|mishap]] that occured, and the top few [[Iotas|iotas]] of the [[The Stack|stack]] right before mishap.

I could hypothetically use it as a method of examining the contents of a [[Casting Items|casting device]] after casting it, although the short backlog of patterns makes getting a complete **Hex** out of anything but the simplest items impossible.

*Now you may only see a pile of receipts, but I see a story. I can see where this story is going. It does not look good.*
