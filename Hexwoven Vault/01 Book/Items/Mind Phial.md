# Mind Phial

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Machina]]

### Mind Phial

*Item:* **Mind Phial**

Getting **[[Media|Media]]** can be hard. After some tinkering it seems that I can [[Expose Mind (Book Entry)|Expose the Mind]] of a creature and I've glimpsed a potential way to get more **Media**.

This Phial is peculiar — it can't be recharged by normal means, but it is bottomless: there is no limit to the amount of **Media** it could store...

Exposing the mind of a creature allows me to extract the **Media** it contains right before its death and store in that strange Phial.

It seems to be 'excited' to collect more and more **Media** this way, somehow getting more **Media** than what would be possible.

After some research the Phial seems to get the most **Media** when its empty, with every consecutive extraction yielding less and less...
