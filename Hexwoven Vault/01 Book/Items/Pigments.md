# Pigments

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Casting]]

The old practitioners of my art sometimes identified themselves by a color, emblematic of them and their **Hexes**. Although their names have faded, their colors remain. It seems a special kind of pigment, offered to Nature in the right way, would "\[...\] paint one's thoughts in a manner pleasing to Nature, inducing a miraculous change in personal colour."

I'm not certain on the specifics, but I believe I have isolated the formulae for many different colors and blends of pigments. To apply a pigment, I hold it in one hand and cast [[Internalize Pigment (Book Entry)|Internalize Pigment]] with the other; this consumes the pigment.

The pigments seem to affect the color of the sparks of **[[Media|media]]** emitted when I cast a **Hex** and my [[Sentinels|sentinel]], but I don't doubt that the color will show up elsewhere.
