# Potions of Wooleyeing

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

These **Potions** let me forget some of the eldritch knowledge **Hexcasting** provides. A base dose removes my ability to trade my own life for **[[Media|media]]**, useful if I do not want to be carried off from a mere miscalculation. Higher potencies remove the ability to cast great spells entirely, possibly useful as an offensive tool.

*It is unseen.*

### Potion of Wooleyeing

*Item:* **Potion**

I can brew one by boiling a [[Periwinkle]] into a simple **Awkward Potion**. **Glowstone Dust** and **Redstone Dust** have their standard amplifying and lengthening effects, as with most potions.
