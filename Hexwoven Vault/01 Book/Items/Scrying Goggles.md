# Scrying Goggles

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Machina]]

### Scrying Goggles

*Item:* **Scrying Goggles**

One [[Scrying Lens]] on its own allows me to see properties of the world and block distractions in my vision when casting hexes.

I expanded the concept into goggles! It doesn't seem that I can see anything that I couldn't with one lens, but it allows me to have better [[Focus|focus]] while casting hexes!
