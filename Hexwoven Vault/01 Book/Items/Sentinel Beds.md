# Sentinel Beds

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

The **Lodestone** inside seems to make this block *and the entities shoved in it* always within my **Hexes**' ambit, although I prefer to believe the **Wool** invites my [[Sentinels|Sentinel]]. Cats seem to find it cozy too, like a lit furnace or a bed.

I hear rumors of ancient **Hexcasters** using this as some kind of [[Iotas|iota]] storage from any range but I struggle to imagine how.
