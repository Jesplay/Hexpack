# Spindles and Pouches

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[YAHA]]

The [[Thought-Knot|Thought Knots]] and [[Focus|Foci]] I've amassed have begun to put a strain on my storage. What was once a manageable handful of string or amethyst, when turned into [[Iotas|iota]]-keeping devices, barely fits inside a large chest. Therefore, I have developed simple tools to bundle my work.

**Thought Knot Spindle**: shaped crafting

|   |
|---|
| `o` |
| `|` |

`o` = Gold Nugget · `|` = Amethyst Shard

A **Spindle** with the capacity of 16 [[Thought-Knot|Thought Knots]].

And here's where I keep assorted lengths of wire.

**Focal Pouch**: shaped crafting

|   |
|---|
| `*` |
| `U` |

`*` = Thought-Knot · `U` = Leather

A **Pouch** with the capacity of 16 [[Focus|Foci]].

These act similarly to **Bundles** for the insertion and removal of items. Right-clicking with the spindle or pouch in my off-hand will eject all stored items onto the ground.

It seems these tools can also be read from with [[Scribe's Reflection]]. What returns is the iota of a randomly chosen [[Thought-Knot|Thought Knot]] or [[Focus]], or null if the chosen item had no iota stored.
