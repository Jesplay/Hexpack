# Staves

> **Category:** [[Items (Category)|Items]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Casting]]

A **Staff** is my entry point into casting all **Hexes**, large and small. By holding it and pressing Right-Click, I begin casting a **Hex**; then I can click and drag to draw patterns.

It's little more than a chunk of **[[Media|media]]** on the end of a stick; that's all that's needed, after all.

**Oak Staff**, **Spruce Staff**, **Birch Staff**, **Jungle Staff**, **Acacia Staff**, **Dark Oak Staff**, **Crimson Staff**, **Warped Staff**, **Mangrove Staff**, **Bamboo Staff**, **Cherry Staff**, **Edified Staff**: shaped crafting (same layout)
