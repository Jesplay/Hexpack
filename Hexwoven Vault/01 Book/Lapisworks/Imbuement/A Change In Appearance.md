# A Change In Appearance

> **Category:** Lapisworks › [[Imbuement (Category)|Imbuement]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Lapisworks]]

It appears that via **Amel** imbuement, I am capable of increasing the power of certain enchantments on **Enchanted Books** beyond their normal limits (basically, I may "supercharge" the **Enchanted Books**). Once this occurs, the book takes on a new appearance due to the latent enchantment energy overwhelming the pages, and the [[Media|media]] forcing it in unlocking more size by infusing with the book itself. This new form may store supercharged enchantments, but otherwise acts just as a normal **Enchanted Book**.

### Enchanted Book

*Item:* **Enchanted Book**

If I find myself fancying this new look, I may use **Mold Amel** to swap between a regular **Enchanted Book** and its "super" form. This transformation is free since the look is merely a biproduct of what happens on the inside and may be mimicked, but due to that very reason, swapping to the former from the latter is impossible if the book contains supercharged enchantments.
