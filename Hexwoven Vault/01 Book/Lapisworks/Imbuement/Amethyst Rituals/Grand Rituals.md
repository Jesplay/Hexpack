# Grand Rituals

> **Category:** Lapisworks › Imbuement › [[Amethyst Rituals (Category)|Amethyst Rituals]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Lapisworks]]

A **Grand Ritual** is a 3x3 multi-block ritual on any surface (such as a wall, the ceiling, or the floor). I can create one if I Right-Click chalk on the middle of a 3x3 collection of chalk (which have space for patterns on them). To write a pattern, I must [[Chalk|stamp]] one onto it.

To activate it, I must Right-Click the **Grand Ritual** with a clear hand. It will prepare then cast a pattern for only half of it's usual cost, using the conditions ([[The Stack|stack]], etcetera) of my [[Staves|staff]]. If I Right-Click it with **Amel**, the **Grand Ritual** disappears after casting.
