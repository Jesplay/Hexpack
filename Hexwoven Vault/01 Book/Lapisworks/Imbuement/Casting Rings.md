# Casting Rings

> **Category:** Lapisworks › [[Imbuement (Category)|Imbuement]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Lapisworks]]

One of the main limitations that has kept me from making a ring to cast with is that the ring would be much too small to even work. However, now that I'm reviewing these texts I can see a path through. Some materials apparently have special interactions with [[Media|media]]. Two examples of such materials are documented on the next page.

**Diamond** focuses media (due to its "chemical structure" (what is that?)) while **Gold** to media (and Enchantment Energy) is like air, letting it freely conduct. The "**Lapisworkers**" of old took these materials and combined them (with **Amel** at times) to make many a great constructs, but many of those have been lost to time. However I can easily create my own.

### Casting Ring

**Ring of Amethyst Lazuli**: shaped crafting

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
| `A` | `G` | `G` |
| `G` | `D` | `G` |
| `G` | `G` | `M` |

`A` = Charged Amethyst · `G` = Gold Ingot · `D` = Diamond · `M` = any amel

**Diamond** to [[Focus|focus]] the media, **Gold** to not block the flow, and **Amel** to attract. I can **keys.lapisworks.use_casting_ring** to open the casting grid when it is equipped.
