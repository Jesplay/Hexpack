# Trinkets

> **Category:** Lapisworks › [[Imbuement (Category)|Imbuement]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Lapisworks]]

The old **Lapisworkers** seemed to have a fondness for making **Trinkets**! Some of their scrolls contain mention of using these as gifts to other people to do many things, including showing appreciation, comforting during times of hardship, etcetera. Perhaps that is why they chose materials a little on the expensive side.

I will document any mentions of these trinkets I find here.

### Focus Necklace

*Item:* **Focus Necklace**

A **[[Focus|Focus]] Necklace**, also called an **Amulet Of Holding** (names useable interchangeably) is a trinket that one wears around their neck. It can be interacted with like a **Focus** via [[Scribe's Reflection|Scribe's Reflection/Gambit]], but it also has it's own patterns that were discovered shortly after it's creation, which allow me to read and write to it while I'm wearing it.

**Focus Necklace**: shaped crafting

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
| `G` |   | `G` |
| `G` | `A` | `G` |
|   | `F` |   |

`G` = Gold Nugget · `A` = any amel · `F` = Focus

*I believe the Amel is put there to attract [[Media|media]] and allow easy access to the focus.*

### Necklace of Undying

*Item:* **Necklace of Undying**

**Totems of Undying** are extremely useful artifacts, talismen of unimaginable power that allows one to survive death. There was a way to, however making them was a taboo art form that has been entirely lost to everyone but the **Evokers** in their mansions. All I have recovered is the method to making a **Necklace of Undying**. It functions as a three-use, wearable, *rechargeable* **Totem of Undying** -- with the caveat that it does not grant me any effects after saving me.

**Necklace of Undying**: shaped crafting

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
| `G` |   | `G` |
| `A` | `T` | `A` |
| `A` | `A` | `A` |

`G` = Gold Nugget · `A` = any amel · `T` = Totem Of Undying

*To repair a single use costs 64 **Amel** and 20 **Charged Amethyst**.*

### Collars

*Item:* **Collar**

**Collars** are a special kind of trinket. The **Collar** itself is a dyeable base that I may put additions on to give interesting properties. Tales, diaries, and journals show that the **Lapisworkers** of the past put it on their pets or, at times, *themselves or other people.*

**Collar**: shaped crafting

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|   | `C` |   |
| `L` | `L` | `L` |
|   | `A` |   |

`C` = Charged Amethyst · `L` = Leather · `A` = any amel

*I believe the **Charged Amethyst** and **Amel** are what allow additions to stick so easily.*

Following are some additions I have found. To remove any, I may hold the item used to craft them in my other hand and hold Right-Click on the collar.

### Bell

*Item:* **Gold Ingot**

I can craft a **Collar** with a **Gold Ingot** to add a bell to it. This bell rings when the wearer moves, and the pitch changes based on their speed. It's quite cute, really.

### Iota Holder

*Item:* **Focus**

Adding a **Focus** to a **Collar** gives it the ability to store iotas. Any entity that wears such a collar becomes a valid target for [[Chronicler's Purification]] and [[Chronicler's Purification|Chronicler's Gambit]], and if I decide to wear one, I may use [[Jeweller's Reflection]] and [[Jeweller's Gambit]] to access it.

### Stealth

*Item:* **Charged Amethyst**

**Charged Amethyst** enhances the media-philic ability of the collar, sucking in the patterns that appear around me when I cast a hex, as well as all particles and sounds produced by [[Flight|flight]]. The collar also becomes invisible when I am invisible.
