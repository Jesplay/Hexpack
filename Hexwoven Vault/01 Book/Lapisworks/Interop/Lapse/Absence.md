# Absence

> **Category:** Lapisworks › Interop › [[Lapse (Category)|Lapse]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Lapisworks]]

I've found texts of a particular author with an eccentric style of writing (e.g. calling very Hex Casting-esque things **Hexal**) and interesting things they depict in their texts. They claim they've stumbled upon "**slipways**" that constantly spew entirely [[Media|media]]-made beings called wisps. I have found no evidence of such in my world yet, however. Though if I ever find any of the things they write about, I will make sure to **add-on** some of my own knowledge to this book.
