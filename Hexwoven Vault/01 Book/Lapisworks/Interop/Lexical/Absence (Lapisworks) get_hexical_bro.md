# Absence

> **Category:** Lapisworks › Interop › [[Lexical (Category)|Lexical]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Lapisworks]]

In my travels, I've found a myth of beings so powerful, so strange they sound fictional. "**Genies**". Much mystery and contradiction surrounds them. Obscure texts depict them as wish-granters, others say that is false, some say they are trapped in lamps, while others state that is only partially true. Whatever the case, I will rewrite these pages and **add-on** some info about them in other places once these "**Hexical**" (did the writers not have the word "hexing" in their time?) objects of myth evolve into rumor.
