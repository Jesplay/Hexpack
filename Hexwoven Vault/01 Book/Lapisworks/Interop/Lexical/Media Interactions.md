# Media Interactions

> **Category:** Lapisworks › Interop › [[Lexical (Category)|Lexical]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Lapisworks]]

I've made a discovery. [[Deposit Media]], [[Withdraw Media]], and [[Inner Media Purification|(thing)Inner Media Purification]] work on not only [[Media Jar|Media Jars]], but also [[Rod and Cradle|Item Cradles]] as well. The only catches are that both cannot sustain any **phianglements**, and the [[Rod and Cradle|Item Cradles]] must contain a **[[Media|media]]-containing item** such as a [[Craft Phial (Book Entry)|Phial]] to function in this manner.
