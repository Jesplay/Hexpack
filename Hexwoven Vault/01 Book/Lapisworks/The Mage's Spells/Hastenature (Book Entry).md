# Hastenature

> **Category:** Lapisworks › [[The Mage's Spells (Category)|The Mage's Spells]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Lapisworks]]

This one was created when the mage was extremely adept at pattern infusions with Amel, their final days. They were a master of it, to the point their infusion created extra patterns, or byproducts. The main pattern here was one that was supposed to be one that'd age enemies to death, a horrifying spell the pattern for which - for better or for worse - was burned.

## [[Hastenature|Hastenature]] ⭐ *Great Spell*

`vec, int` → `—`

Takes a block position and a positive number, and ages the block (or how I call it, induces a random tick) there that many times. Costs 1 shard per age-up, but 3.5 if the target is Budding Amethyst.
