# The Ancient Mage

> **Category:** Lapisworks › [[The Mage's Spells (Category)|The Mage's Spells]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Lapisworks]]

I have found rumour of an old, knowledgeable mage that has done much evil and staved off the hordes of vigilantism, yet always had low [[Media|media]] reserves. I've read tale that while they attempted to burn most of their scriptures near their demise, some adventurers and intellectuals had managed to save some of them from the grand fire.

Based off of this information I predict they may have buried the knowledge away or put it in the strongholds I've read of, such as pyramids in the desert, brick structures underground, etcetera.
