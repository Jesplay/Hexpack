# Undocumented Hexwright Patterns

> **Category:** [[Not in the Book (Category)|Not in the Book]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexwright]]

These patterns exist in \*\*Hexwright\*\* (their strokes are in the mod code and their names in its language file), but the mod's book pages don't describe them. Expect them to work, but test them carefully: the inputs and outputs are unknown unless the name makes them obvious.
