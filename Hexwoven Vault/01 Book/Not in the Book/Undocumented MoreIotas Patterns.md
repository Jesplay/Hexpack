# Undocumented MoreIotas Patterns

> **Category:** [[Not in the Book (Category)|Not in the Book]]  
> **Source mod:** [[MoreIotas]]

These patterns exist in \*\*MoreIotas\*\* (their strokes are in the mod code and their names in its language file), but the mod's book pages don't describe them. Expect them to work, but test them carefully: the inputs and outputs are unknown unless the name makes them obvious.

### Names without detectable strokes

The language file also names these, but no strokes were found in the code. They may be removed, disabled or registered in an unusual way: Transpose Purification (moreiotas:matrix/transpose), Concatenation Distillation (moreiotas:string/add), Discoverer's Distillation (moreiotas:string/find), Winnowing Distillation (moreiotas:string/sub).
