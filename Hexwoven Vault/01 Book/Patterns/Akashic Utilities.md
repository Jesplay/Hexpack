# Akashic Utilities

> **Category:** [[Patterns (Category)|Patterns]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

Being designed to be receptive to **[[Media|media]]** and store information, it should not surprise me that there are more conveniences than meets the eye. I can use or [[Prestidigitation (Book Entry)|magically interact]] with a filled [[Akashic Libraries|Bookshelf]] to copy its [[Iotas|iota]] onto my [[The Stack|stack]]. I can also use [[Scrolls|Scroll]] to copy the pattern key onto it. There are also a set of patterns to read and write directly to a [[Akashic Libraries|Bookshelf]] within ambit for free.
