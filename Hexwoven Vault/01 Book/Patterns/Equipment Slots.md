# Equipment Slots

> **Category:** [[Patterns (Category)|Patterns]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexic]]

Nature has a rigid, structured system for the arrangement of equipment on creatures (including me). If I ignore inventory, an item can either be held in one of two hands, worn in one of four locations on the body, or equipped in another well-defined location. For example, I can equip a ring on one of my two ring fingers, but I'm not capable of putting a ring on any other finger, or anywhere else on my body.

There at least 8 types of equipment slots in every universe:
- My left and right arms, which can hold any item
- My four armor slots, which can only hold armor of that type.
- Slots for at least a lens and mediaweave, though there's typically much more of this type (these are typically called ‘trinkets’ or ‘accessories’, although older texts refer to them as ‘curios’).
