# Greater Blink

> **Category:** Patterns › [[Great Spells (Category)|Great Spells]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

Greater Blink is a significantly cheaper alternative to [[Greater Teleport (Book Entry)|Greater Teleport]]. It also does not carry the risk of splattering my items upon arrival. However, it only has pitiful range compared to [[Greater Teleport (Book Entry)|Greater Teleport]] and its destination calculations are... esoteric. Its target is also exclusively myself.

Teleports me up to 128 blocks away, relative to my position and rotation. Costs about two [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]].

A vector of (0, 0, 1) would teleport me one block in [[Alidade's Purification|the direction I'm facing]]. A vector of (0, 1, 0) would teleport me one block [[Theodolite's Purification|"up" from the top of my head]]. A vector of (0, 1, 10) would teleport me ten blocks forward in the direction I'm facing and one block upwards from the top of my head. A vector of (1, 0, 0) teleports me one block to my right.

*I felt three numerical axes perfectly pierce all six faces of my head. For a moment, I was skewered—suspended midair—and then at my specifications, I was violently dragged along the geometrical scaffolds at speeds indistinguishable from instantaneousness.*

*I must have been no longer a part of the world, for I was yanked through walls without causing even the smallest disturbance.*
