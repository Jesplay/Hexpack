Summon a greater version of my [[Sentinels|Sentinel]]. Costs about two [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]].

The stronger [[Sentinels|sentinel]] acts like the normal one I can summon without the use of a Great Spell, if a little more visually interesting. However, the range in which my spells can work is extended to a small region around my greater [[Sentinels|sentinel]], about 16 blocks. In other words, no matter where in the world I am, I can interact with things around my [[Sentinels|sentinel]] (the mysterious forces of chunkloading notwithstanding).
