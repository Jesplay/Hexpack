# Zeniths

> **Category:** Patterns › [[Great Spells (Category)|Great Spells]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Casting]]

This family of spells all impart a positive potion effect upon an entity, similar to the [[Nadirs]]. However, these have their **[[Media|media]]** costs increase with the cube of the potency.
