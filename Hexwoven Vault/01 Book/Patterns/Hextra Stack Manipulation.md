## [[Angler's Gambit|Angler's Gambit]]

`many` → `many`

A family of patterns that takes the Nth iota from the top of the stack, determined by the length of the head, and duplicates it in place. The copy will be created right above the original iota.

The first pattern is identical to [[Gemini Decomposition]].

The second pattern, with a head length of 1, duplicates the first iota from the top. \[1, 0\] becomes \[1, 1, 0\].

The third pattern follows this. \[2, 1, 0\] becomes \[2, 2, 1, 0\].
