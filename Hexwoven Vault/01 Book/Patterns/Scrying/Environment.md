# Environment

> **Category:** Patterns › [[Scrying (Category)|Scrying]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexpose]]

These patterns are meta: they concern properties of the magical environment they are cast in. Mostly useful in avoiding [[Mishaps|mishaps]].
