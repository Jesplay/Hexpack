# Armor Stand Manipulation

> **Category:** Patterns › [[Spells (Category)|Spells]]  
> **Source mod:** [[YAHA]]

The displays of my battle-worn armor, although impressive, are starting to feel... stagnant. With this in mind, I have developed a set of spells to remedy this banality.

The following spells all cost a negligible amount of **[[Media|media]]**.
