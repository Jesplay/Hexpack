# Caster's Armaments

> **Category:** Patterns › [[Spells (Category)|Spells]]  
> **Source mod:** [[YAHA]]

I've devised a set of **[[Media|media]]**-borne weaponry to improve upon my offensive Hexes in flair, if not in power.

Unlike most projectiles, [[Alidade's Purification]] is consistent with the velocity of the projectiles conjured by these spells.

`vector, entity` → `entity`

Summons a projectile that slowly flies towards the given entity, dealing 3 hearts of damage. Costs one [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]]. Adds itself onto the [[The Stack|stack]].

A sheet of **media** bearing the [[Iotas|iota]] of an entity, folded like a paper plane. The embedded iota coerces the plane towards its target. With such fragile construction, it loses form after about 10 seconds. Planes can also be redirected, much like a fireball, and lose their target upon doing so.

Normally, paper planes pass through one another. However, conjuring a plane with another as a target allows the first to collide with the second. Planes without a target fly in a straight path.

`vec, [pattern], num, num` → `entity`

Summons a floating projectile that casts after a given duration. Costs one [[Amethyst|Charged Amethyst]], plus the **media** given to the bomb. Adds itself onto the stack.

A hex suspended in a fluid of **media** and encased in clear amethyst.

The required arguments are as follows:
- The position of the bomb
- A list of patterns to be cast by the bomb
- The amount of **media** in units of [[Amethyst|Dust]] given to the bomb's cast
- The duration before casting, in ticks

The bomb casts as the player that produced it, has an ambit of 8 blocks around it, and can be knocked back by attacks and other projectiles. The bomb entity still exists during its cast and disintegrates after, which must be considered when dealing with [[Entities|Zone Distillations]].

A slow, whorling current of violet pearlescence.
It is entrancing - more than any other device I have been given yet
