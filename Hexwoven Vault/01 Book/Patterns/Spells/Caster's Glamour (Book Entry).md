Certain items I create seem oddly receptive to the influence of **[[Media|media]]**. By holding a [[Casting Items|Cypher]], [[Casting Items|Trinket]], [[Casting Items|Artifact]], [[Focus]], or [[Spellbook]] in my other hand, I can use this spell to change the appearance of the item. Costs a negligible amount of **media**.
