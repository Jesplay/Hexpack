# Circle Spells

> **Category:** Patterns › [[Spells (Category)|Spells]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

When the **[[Media|media]]** courses through the boundaries of a [[Spell Circles|circle]], it saturates the domain inside with **media**, allowing far greater magical effects and flexibility of **media** manipulation than I am used to. The following are some spells which rely on that increased density of **media** or properties of the [[Spell Circles|circle]] itself and are to only be cast by an [[Impeti|Impetus]].
