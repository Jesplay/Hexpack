`Vector, itemtype` → `—`

Accepts a vector and an itemtype representing a [[Pigments|pigment]]. Costs a negligible amount of [[Media|media]] if targeting my own block, or 1 Amethyst Dust if not.

Sets the colors of the conjured block corresponding to the vector to the colorset represented by the passed pigment, or mine if passed a NULL [[Iotas|iota]].

Not guaranteed to work with unconventional forms of conjured matter.

Feels strangely celebratory, as if something similar was once used in grand festivals where teams would splatter their colors all over their opponents' territory in a bid for ideological dominance.
