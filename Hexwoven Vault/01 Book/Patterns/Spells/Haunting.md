# Haunting

> **Category:** Patterns › [[Spells (Category)|Spells]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Special Efhexs]]

I have discovered various spells for creating [[Particles (Book Entry)|particles]] and [[Sounds|sounds]] but initially, these are plainly visible to everyone. What if instead, I wanted to only create effects for myself or haunt a specific player? That's where this pattern comes in. For the remainder of the **Hex**, I can choose only certain players to be made privy.
