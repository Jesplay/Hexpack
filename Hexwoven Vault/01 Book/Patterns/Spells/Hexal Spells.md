`vec | entity | mote` → `—`

Remove a location, item entity, or mote from the [[The Stack|stack]], then either smelt the block at the location or smelt all the items in the item entity or mote.

Costs three quarters of an [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]] per item smelted (i.e. three quarters of an [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]] for smelting a block, 6 [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]] to smelt a stack of 8 items.)

Freezes the block at a block location. For example, turns water into ice, and ice into packed ice. Costs one [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]].

`(itemtype | mote), vec` → `—`

Accepts an item type or mote and a location, and attempts to place a block of that item type at that location. If a mote was passed it will instead place from the record referenced by that mote. Costs an eighth of an [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]].
