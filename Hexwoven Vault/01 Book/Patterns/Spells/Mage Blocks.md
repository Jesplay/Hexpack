# Mage Blocks

> **Category:** Patterns › [[Spells (Category)|Spells]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

The blocks from [[Conjure Block]] last forever, are consequently a pain to clean up, and are not too versatile. Luckily, Nature offers an elegant and flexible alternative. These **Mage Blocks** come with a suite of *modifiers*. I could even [[The Stack|stack]] multiple modifiers on one block and analyze its composition with a [[Scrying Lens]].
