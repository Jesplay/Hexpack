# Projectiles

> **Category:** Patterns › [[Spells (Category)|Spells]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

While conjuring projectiles from pure **[[Media|media]]** manipulation should be prohibitively expensive, Nature offers a set of cheaper spells to create certain projectiles.

These spells push the conjured entity to the [[The Stack|stack]], which I must manually [[Impulse|propel]] in my desired direction.

*Can I offer you a nice egg in this trying time?*
