# Telepathy

> **Category:** [[Patterns (Category)|Patterns]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

**[[Media|Media]]** is the energy of thought and consequently, it can interact with thoughts as well. Unfortunately or perhaps fortunately for me, the minds of **Hexcasters** are too resistant to be swayed by **Hexes** and even the minds of villagers resist tampering. There is a notable exception though: my own mind is open to my own **media**, allowing me to read my own intentions and write thoughts to myself.
