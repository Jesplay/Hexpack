# Ambit Extenders

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Slate Works]]

### Ambit Extender

*Item:* **Ambit Extender**

The **Ambit Extender** is a fabulous answer to my **Ambit** woes when it comes to [[Spell Circles]]. This pops a vector from the [[The Stack|stack]], and extends ambit in the direction (negative vectors extending the negative corner of Circle Ambit).

*With great range comes with great costs, err-*

While this is not as strong as... [other](https://forum.petra-k.at/viewtopic.php?t=136) methods of giving a [[Spell Circles|Spell Circle]] more **Ambit**, I do feel like Nature takes a kinder approach to my amalgamation of free will if I use this.

This does not come cheap however, the **[[Media|media]]** cost increases with the square of the total distance extended in [[Amethyst|dust]].

**Ambit Extender**: shaped crafting

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
| `B` | `A` | `B` |
| `A` | `D` | `A` |
| `B` | `A` | `B` |

`B` = Block of Slate · `A` = Shard of Quenched Allay · `D` = any directrices

Since the **Ambit Extender** operates on **Ambit**, having a "core" block that can operate on **Media Waves** would be beneficial. As well, having nearly [[Quenching Allays|Pure Media]] would also be required for this construction.
