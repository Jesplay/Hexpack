# Bombastic Extractor

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Slate Works]]

As far as my works has come, I have yet found a way to efficiently break a block. Nature has given me the [[Break Block]] spell, yes; however, it can not apply things suck as **Fortune** nor **Silk Touch**, acting as a plain **Diamond Pickaxe**. This is why shortly after my [[The Work|grand awakening]], I quickly jotted down plans for this: the **Bombastic Extractor**.

*...Pardon the pun.*

### Bombastic Extractor

*Item:* **Bombastic Extractor**

Due to the mixture of **Netherite** compounds, the **Bombastic Extractor** harvests at a Netherite level. As well, this can be enchanted with pickaxe specific enchantments, allowing it to harvest with either **Fortune** or **Silk Touch** (along with **Efficiency** for lower costs).

Importantly, the cost of the **Bombastic Extractor** changes based on the enchantments. Firstly, the base cost per block is 1/8th of a [[Amethyst|dust]], much like [[Break Block]]. **Fortune** increases the cost by a [[Amethyst|dust]] per level, while **Silk Touch** increases the cost by a flat [[Amethyst|shard]]. Finally, efficiency decreases the cost by 1.5 times per level.

Interestingly, this construct doesn't just harvest the block in front; instead, it reaches from its current point to the end of [[Spell Circles|Spell Circle]]. And rather than indiscriminately excavating blocks, the **Bombastic Extractor** uses (but not consumes) a **Hex**. This **Hex** is ran over each positon, providing the **Hex** the [[Items|type]] of the position (much like [[Thoth's Gambit]]). Which if the **Hex** returns True, the block is harvested; if False, the block is ignored.

Finally, the **Bombastic Extractor** when breaking blocks, also collects them into all activated [[Storage Vessels]]. If the activated [[Storage Vessels]] are full, the broken block falls to the ground normally.
