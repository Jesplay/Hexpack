# Extended Fanciful Staves

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]

Just as my perception has risen to see a new horizon, it is only right that the dazzling end of my [[Staves|staff]] see the same. It is only right that it follows in the *length* of my wit, my stature, my influence, *my power*. It is only right.
