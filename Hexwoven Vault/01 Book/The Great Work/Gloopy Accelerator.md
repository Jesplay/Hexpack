# Gloopy Accelerator

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Slate Works]]

After diving into libraries of old, I discovered blueprints for something called an "Accelerator," which was described as speeding up [[Spell Circles|Spell Circle]] greatly. After some time of reverse engineering some materials lost to time, I have recreated it as the **Gloopy Accelerator**!

How the **Gloopy Accelerator** works exactly, is it takes 2 [[Amethyst|dust]] to speed the [[Spell Circles|Circle]] up to a slate per 1/20th of a second for 13 blocks; however, it needs to spend some of those 13 blocks speeding up. Interestingly, I can "[[The Stack|stack]]" their boosts, making the accelerated state last for much longer. But this also increases the cost by the square of the blocks to be accelerated as well. So I must be careful when spacing them out.

**Gloopy Accelerator**: shaped crafting

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
| `I` | `B` | `I` |
| `J` | `A` | `J` |
| `S` | `S` | `S` |

`I` = Copper Ingot · `B` = Copper Block · `J` = Slime Ball · `A` = Quenched Allay · `S` = Block of Slate

While I could not recreate the lost material of "gloop," a mixture of slime and [[Quenching Allays|Quenched Allay]] seems to emulate it well enough.
