# Merchant Directrix

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Slate Works]]

The Villagers are... interesting creatures that I must use to fuel my [[On The Flaying of Minds|endeavors]]; but talking and using them still is a highly manual task. But with the methods I have developed, I now may automatically trade with them and train their minds with [[Spell Circles]] by using a **Merchant Directrix**.

### Merchant Directrix

*Item:* **Merchant Directrix**

The **Merchant Directrix** are powerful. Since they may [[Exchange Mind|obtain]] a villager's Job by swaping their internal Job with the villager's Job. This the allows for the Job to be interacted with purely and efficiently, without the baggage of the fleshy being.

Unfortunately, the **Merchant Directrix** do have some of their own stipulations. To be used, the **[[Media|Media]] Wave** of a [[Spell Circles|Spell Circle]] must active the **Directrix** while carrying a whole number between 0 and how many trades are held within the **Directrix**. Once a trade is attempted, there are 2 outcomes: it was a successes, and leaves via the "nose" side; or it failed (needs to restock, not enough items, etc), and leaves via the angry face.

Additionally, **Merchant Directrixs** pull from and deposit too the [[Storage Vessels]]. Trading seems to "level up" the Job as well, letting them gain new trades and strengthening the stored Intelligence.
As for the final stipulation, I do not know how, but the Jobs can still become exhausted, and require time to "restock." At both dawn and dusk, the trades will refresh and restock; though annoyingly the prices do seem to reflect on my trading habits.

If I adorn a [[Scrying Lens]] and look at the **Merchant Directrix**, I may see: the Job it contains, all the trades, prices, how many trades have been conducted since last restock, and how many trade the **Directrix** can do.
For my final notes: if I were to accidentally slip and break the **Directrix**, it will keep the Job tucked away safely; and it wont be lost.
