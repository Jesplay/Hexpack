# Modified Slates

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Ephemera]]

I've designed a couple types of slate to be used in [[Spell Circles|spell circles]]. While they don't provide any groundbreaking new functionality, they can be nice to have.

### Inert Slate

*Item:* **Inert Slate**

Crafted with a normal slate and a stonecutter.

Looks just like a normal slate, but can't direct a [[Media|media]] wave at all. Cannot be written to.
