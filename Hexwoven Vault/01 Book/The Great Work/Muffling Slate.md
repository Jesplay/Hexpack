# Muffling Slate

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Slate Works]]

### Muffling Slate

*Item:* **Muffling Slate**

While I work with [[Spell Circles]] more and more, the constant *crackles* from the **[[Media|Media]] Wave** has begun to ring in my ears. And with some of my more... esoteric [[Spell Circles|Circles]], nearly deafening me.

Luckily, this, the **Muffling Slate**, can protect my ears from the obnoxious sounds and noises.

When activated by a [[Spell Circles|Spell Circle]], this pops a number between 1 and 0 (inclusive for both), and sets the "volume" of the crackles and rackets to that. This applies to all sounds that the **Media Wave** makes, so executing patterns, [[Spell Imprinter|Macros]], or moving will be silenced.

*"Nat, he's made sound! He's an audiomage!"*
*"I hear it," Nat replies, not looking back. "And stop inventing words."*

**Muffling Slate**: shapeless crafting from any wool, Slate

Thankfully, the **Muffling Slate** is a cheap and simple craft; taking a scrap of wool, and some slate.

*Silence is gold for some, or even iron for more; but for my self, silence is diamonds.*
