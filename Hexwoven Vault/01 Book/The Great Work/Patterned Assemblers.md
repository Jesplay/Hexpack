# Patterned Assemblers

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Slate Works]]

### Patterned Assembler

*Item:* **Patterned Assembler**

Oh how my hands *Ache* and *Buzz* from countless hours of slaving over a **Crafting Table**... luckily these days are over. The **Patterned Assembler** solves these pains and problems.

*Items ever deeper in items, I cant stop crafting, I cant stop looking; more items must be crafted and automated. **NOW***.

Precisely what the **Patterned Assembler** does is it attempts to craft whatever item is set within, pulling from activated [[Storage Vessels]], and placing the resulting item(s) back into the **Vessels**. This is triggered when the **Assembler** is activated with a [[Spell Circles|Spell Circle]]. The recipe can be set by hand, or via a [[Set Recipe|Spell]]. If it can craft the inscribed item, it returns a True to the [[The Stack|stack]] and crafts the item, else it does not craft the item (not enough ingredients, no recipe, etc) and returns a False.

While the **Patterned Assembler** can be quite quick, it can still take an agonizingly long time to finish large crafting orders. Thus, this is why I have created [[Set Craft Count|patterns]] for "bulk operations." This lets the **Patterned Assembler** try to craft more than one craft per activation; but, if the **Patterned Assembler** can't craft all the items at once, it will push a False onto the stack, and crafting nothing.

**Patterned Assembler**: [[Flay Mind]] a **librarian** villager, level 2+ into **Crafting Table**

The **Patterned Assembler** requires the mind of a villager well adapted to paging through tomes and texts; it seems like the **Librarian** is perfect for this.
