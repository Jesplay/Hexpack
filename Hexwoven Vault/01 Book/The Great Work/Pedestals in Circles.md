# Pedestals in Circles

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hexical]]

[[Pedestals]] have magical abilities!

If they are holding [[Scribe's Gambit|an item that contains information]] and the [[Spell Circles|spell circle]]'s wave passes over them as if they were a [[Slates|Slate]], they push the [[Iotas|iota]] onto the [[The Stack|stack]]. If the [[Pedestals|Pedestal]] happens to processed inside an unclosed [[Introspection]], the iota appears inside the constructed list.

An [[Spell Circles|Impetus]] can also [[Appendage|use]] a [[Pedestals|Pedestal]] as its *other hand*, useful for spells such as the [[Crafting Casting Items|casting device crafting spells]], [[Recharge Item]], and [[Erase Item]].

This is useful for creating automated constructions to [[Recharge Item|recharge]] my [[Casting Items|casting devices]].
