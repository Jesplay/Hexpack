# Pocket Simulator

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Slate Works]]

As I strive to expand my domain, I found it mind *numbingly* boring to lug items around in my pockets and bags. And whilst **Hexes** help automate this, I find my self requiring to hold items still... I must engineer a better solution to this; it seems like the **Allays** can hold items, perhaps I can use that?

### Pocket Simulator

*Item:* **Pocket Simulator**

The **Pocket Simulator** solves my inventory woes!
Using an **Allay** as the core of it, it can hold up to 6 "stacks" of items, playfully dancing around the **Simulator**. As well, I can denote 1 of those stacks to being a [[Working with Items|held item]], meaning blockwork spells will pull from that first.

Once a [[Spell Circles|Spell Circle]] activates this, it becomes "bound," being able to use the **Simulator**.

Wonderfully, the **Pocket Simulator** can be interacted via my hands, or [[Pocket Simulator Patterns|spells]]. If I interact with it via my hands, I am greeted with the 6 slots it stores, and the held item slot will be glowing with [[Quenching Allays|Quenched Allay]] colors.

A fascinating discovery is that the **Pocket Simulator** seems to function much like common [[Working with Items|item picking behaviors]]. This allows the **Pocket Simulator** to "hold" items, thus when [[Block Manipulation|block spells]] attempt to pull an item, they will pull from the [[Storage Vessels]] instead of the **Pocket Simulator**.

Important to note, this also functions just as a plain held item. Thus letting the [[Spell Circles|Spell Circle]] read from [[Focus|Foci]] (and other [[Iotas|iota]] holding items) as well as pull **[[Media|media]]** from phials.
