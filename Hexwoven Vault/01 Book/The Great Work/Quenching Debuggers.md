# Quenching Debuggers

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[HexDebug]]

My mind has been apotheosized, but my [[Debugging Items|tools]] are still so limited, bursting at the seams with the enormity of the Work. I need -- no, DESERVE -- more.

The shards of an Allay make an excellent medium for the task. As they twist and jump around, so too can these tools -- holding **Sneak** and **Sprint** while scrolling will switch between slots for **Hexes** to debug.

**Quenched Debugger**: flyswatter quenching (see the in-game book / JEI)

**Quenched Evaluator**: flyswatter quenching (see the in-game book / JEI)

These enlightened tools also synergize nicely with other [[Spell Circles|great works]]. I can use a **Quenched Debugger** on an [[Impeti|Impetus]] to take control of the wave of **[[Media|media]]**, holding it back according to my will. For certain types of [[Impeti]], I may also have to initially hold **Sneak** for my command to be recognized.

I can even control multiple [[Spell Circles|spell circles]] at once, so long as I remain close enough to the relevant [[Impeti|Impetus]] when actively advancing it.

This ability seems to be extraordinarily useful for finding problems in my grand assemblies, something the ancients could never have done so easily with their primitive tools.

Finally, I suppose I should note that a regular, unquenched [[Debugging Items|Debugger]] can also be used for this purpose, but why would I limit myself to such crude and unsophisticated methods?
