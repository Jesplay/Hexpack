# Spell Circles

> **Category:** [[The Great Work (Category)|The Great Work]]  
> **Source mod:** [[Hex Casting]]

I KNOW what the [[Slates|slates]] are for. The grand assemblies lost to time. The patterns scribed on them can be actuated in sequence, automatically. Thought and power ricocheting through, one by one by one by one by one by through and through and THROUGH AND -- I must not I must not I should know better than to think that way.

To start the ritual I need an [[Impeti|Impetus]] to create a self-sustaining wave of **[[Media|media]]**. That wave travels along a track of [[Slates|slates]] or other suitable blocks, casting any patterns it finds.

If there is no way for the wave to return to the [[Impeti|Impetus]], the casting will fail immediately. Also, the direction the wave exits any given block MUST be unambiguous, or the casting will fail at the block with too many neighbors.

As a result, the outline of the spell "circle" may be any closed shape, concave or convex, and it may face any direction. In fact, with the application of certain other blocks it is possible to make a spell circle that spans all three dimensions. I doubt such an oddity has very much use, but I must allocate myself a bit of vapid levity to encourage my crude mind to continue my work.

Miracle of miracles, the circle will withdraw **media** neither from my inventory nor my mind. Instead, crystallized shards of **media** must be provided to the [[Impeti|Impetus]] via hopper, or other such artifice.

The application of a [[Scrying Lens]] will show how much **media** is inside an [[Impeti|Impetus]], in units of dust.

However, a spell cast from a circle does have one major limitation: it is unable to affect anything outside of the circle's bounds. That is, it cannot interact with anything outside of the cuboid of minimum size which encloses every block composing it (so a concave spell circle can still affect things in the concavity).

There is also a limit on the number of blocks the wave can travel through before it disintegrates, but it is large enough I doubt I will have any trouble.

Conversely, there are some actions that can only be cast from a circle. Fortunately, none of them are spells; they all seem to deal with components of the circle itself. My notes on the subject are [[Spell Circle Patterns|here]].

I also found a sketch of a spell circle used by the ancients buried in my notes. Facing this page is my (admittedly poor) copy of it.

The patterns there would have been executed counter-clockwise, starting with [[Mind's Reflection]] and ending with [[Greater Teleport]].
