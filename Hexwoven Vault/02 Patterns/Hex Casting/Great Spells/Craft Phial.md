## Description

Infuse a bottle with **[[Media|media]]** to form a [[Phials of Media|Phial.]]

Similarly to the spells for [[Crafting Casting Items]], I must hold a **Glass Bottle** in my other hand, and provide the spell with a dropped [[The Stack|stack]] of [[Amethyst]]. See [[Phials of Media|this page]] for more information.

Costs about one [[Amethyst|Charged Amethyst]].

## Example

Makes a [[Phials of Media|Phial of Media]], a rechargeable media battery your hexes draw from (and it gives exact change, so no wasted amethyst). **Hold a Glass Bottle in your other hand**, drop a stack of amethyst on the ground, target that dropped amethyst (e.g. [[Entity Purification Item]] at its position), then cast. Costs about one Charged Amethyst; refill later with [[Recharge Item]].
