## Description

Summon a block of lava (or insert up to a bucket's worth) into a block at the given position. Costs about one [[Amethyst|Charged Amethyst]].

It may be advisable to keep my knowledge of this spell secret. A certain faction of botanists get... touchy about it, or so I've heard.

Well, no one said tracing the deep secrets of the universe was going to be an easy time.

## Example

Places a lava source block at a position: the [[Archer's Distillation]] opener (plus [[Architect's Distillation]] if you want it in front of the face) → **Create Lava**.
