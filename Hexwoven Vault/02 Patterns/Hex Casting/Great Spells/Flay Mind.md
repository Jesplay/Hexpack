## Description

I cannot make heads or tails of this spell... To be honest, I'm not sure I want to know what it does.

## Example

**What it actually does:** pulls the mind out of a villager (or allay) and fuses it into a block, turning that block into something new: [[Impeti|impetuses]], [[Directrices|directrices]], Akashic Records, Budding Amethyst, Hexal's [[Mote Nexus & Motes|Mote Nexus]] and more. The villager dies. Costs **10 Charged Amethyst**.

**How:** villager next to the target block → look at the villager: opener + [[Scout's Distillation]] → look at the block: opener + [[Archer's Distillation]] → **Flay Mind**. The [[The Stack|stack]] must be `villager, block-pos`.

Needs **enlightenment** and the per-world strokes from a scroll. Full walkthrough plus a table of every recipe in the pack: [[Progression - Enlightenment & Great Spells]].
