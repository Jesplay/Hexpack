## Description

Binds the given demiplane to a position in my current dimension (which must not be the demiplane itself). This position is used when exiting the demiplane, as well as in case of any *unfortunate accidents*. Costs one shard.

My mind is drifting drifting *spinning*, *running* from me with every slice I take of Nature. Every passing moment my grip oh my hands weakening my mind passing through like sandI must steady myself. The price of sanity is outright shattering the planes I've made — a burst of [[Media|media]] worth 25 shards, and the pocket will crumple like a villager, spewing its contents into where it was bound.Testing this process on living creatures is unwise.
