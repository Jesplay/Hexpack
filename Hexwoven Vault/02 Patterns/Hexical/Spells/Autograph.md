## Description

Autographs the item in my offhand with my name and [[Pigments|Pigment]]. Autographing an item with my name already on it moves my name to the top of the list.

Because an autograph is strongly imprinted on an item, [[Erase Item]] is insufficient to remove it; a specialized spell is required to extripate autographs.

However, a spell capable of doing by its very nature will also destroy a [[Focus]]'s contents or a [[Casting Items|casting device]]'s **Hex** and **[[Media|media]]**. Perhaps that is for the better—my autographed **Hexcasting** gifts can be uniquely assured to be untampered.
