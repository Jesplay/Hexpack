## Description

This spell vanishes the [[The Stack|stack]] of items in my other hand, or conjures it back, swapping if my other hand is holding something. Costs about a eighth of one [[Amethyst|Amethyst Dust]].

While the items disappear from reality in almost all measurable aspects, certain items' effects can still function even when hidden in the wristpocket.

I hear of certain **Totems** that still function within the wristpocket. [[Phials of Media|Phials]] also can still provide for my **Hexes** within the wristpocket.
