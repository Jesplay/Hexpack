```text
before   … │ vec │ vec │ vec │ vec  ◂ top

```

## Description

Takes the position & normal of the source storage and the position & normal of the sink storage and creates a [[Media|media]] conduit.
Costs 36 Charged Amethyst to weave the conduit no matter its length.
