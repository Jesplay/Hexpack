## Description

[[Muninn's Reflection]], but for an additional mind. A mind you have never written to reflects null.

The additional minds behave exactly as the ravenmind does. Minds survive from one pattern to the next within a cast, and across a whole [[Staves|staff]] casting session, Talismans also have access to the extra minds, they are blank each time the Talisman hex fires.
