## Description

Cycle through the appearances I've designed of the **Amel** or **Amel-infused Construct** (such as an **Amel Ring**) in my other hand. (e.g. **Amel** has four forms.)

The next patterns enchant various parts of my body, costing me in **Amel** (from any equipped trinkets, then my hands and then from my hotbar) and [[Media|media]] proportional to the change in enchantment. Enchanting over the limit clamps to said limit. If I specify an enchantment level below the current, it disenchants to that level for no **Amel** (but retains the media cost). Provided enhancements stay with me for the rest of my life.
