## Description

Accepts an entity and a Spatial Imprint and teleports the entity to its position in the proper dimension. Generally costs 20 Charged Amethyst.
Will fail if supplied a player other than myself.

If I use this to return to a dimension that I previously left within the same instant, the cost drops to five amethyst dust, as the spell can take advantage of a lingering strand of [[Media|media]] leading back to my original position. Otherwise, it will attempt to find a safe spot to deposit me, potentially conjuring a block to prevent me from falling to my doom.

Strangely, I feel an odd sense of acceleration when transporting myself this way, along some incomprehensible axis.
