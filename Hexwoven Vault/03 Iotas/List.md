# List

An ordered collection of iotas. Lists of patterns are how you store and execute whole hexes as data.

> [!example] Example
> `[1, 2, 3]` or a whole hex: [[Introspection]] … [[Retrospection]] makes a list of patterns.

See also: [[Iotas]]
