## Reflection

**Reflections push one new iota without consuming anything.** They're how you *get* information into a hex: who you are ([[Mind's Reflection]]), constants ([[Arc's Reflection]] = π, [[Vacant Reflection]] = empty list), numbers ([[Numerical Reflection]]).
**Useful for:** starting a hex, getting constants, reading state (stack size, ravenmind, focus contents).

## Gambit

**Gambits are the "do something clever" patterns.** They don't follow a fixed in/out shape. Instead they **rearrange the stack** (swap, rotate, copy, delete), **control how the hex runs** (run a list of patterns, loop, stop early), or **save data** somewhere outside the stack. See **[[Gambits Explained]]** for a full guide.
**Useful for:** everything beyond a straight line of patterns: loops, reusable hexes, getting arguments into the right order.

## Escaping

**Escaping patterns turn drawing into data.** [[Consideration]] escapes the next pattern; [[Introspection]] … [[Retrospection]] records everything in between as a list; [[Evanition]] undoes the last recorded pattern. They're how you build lists of patterns to store, pass to [[Hermes' Gambit]] or loop with [[Thoth's Gambit]]. See [[Escaping Patterns]] and [[Advanced Escaping]].
